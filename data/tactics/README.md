# Scam Manipulation Tactics (`data/tactics/`)

## Intended Purpose
Granular repository of psychological manipulation techniques observed in Indian scams:
- Urgency & Fear (Electricity cut, SIM deactivation, Digital Arrest)
- Authority Impersonation (CBI, Cyber Crime, Police, TRAI, Banks)
- Greed & Opportunity (Part-time YouTube task, Investment doubling, Lottery)
- Sympathy & Relationship Grooming (Romance, Fake parcel customs)

## Governance Rules
1. Documented with pattern regexes, keyword lexicons, and linguistic dialect variations (English, Hindi, Hinglish).
