# Investigation Workspace Specification

## 1. Tri-Pane Analyst Workbench Layout
The `/investigate` workspace is structured into three coordinated panes:
1. **Left Pane (Query & Input Composer)**: Multi-signal composer supporting text, URLs, phone numbers, UPI VPAs, and multimodal presets (Screenshot OCR, Audio Call, UPI QR).
2. **Center Pane (Assessment & Journey View)**: Live risk gauge, journey stage progression, detected manipulation tactics, and linguistic breakdown.
3. **Right Pane (Evidence & Graph Context)**: Subsystem signal weights, entity reputation cards, and pre-payment guard intervention guidance.
