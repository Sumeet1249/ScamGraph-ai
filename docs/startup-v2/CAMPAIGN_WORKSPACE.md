# Campaign Detail Workspace Specification

## 1. Multi-Tab Analyst Investigation Surface
The `/campaigns/[id]` workspace provides operational depth across 10 specialized investigative tabs:

1. **Overview**: Executive summary, syndicate hypothesis, velocity trends, and key risk indicators.
2. **Timeline**: Historical activity graph showing incident arrivals and lifecycle transitions over time.
3. **Journey**: Reconstructed aggregate scam workflow stages, monetization boundaries, and friction exit points.
4. **ScamDNA Profile**: 8-dimensional behavioral spider profile distinguishing missing dimensions from zero scores.
5. **Network Topology**: Subgraph visualization of linked infrastructure, shared bank handles, and phone numbers.
6. **Entities**: Exhaustive canonical entity table with reputation states and observation counts.
7. **Incidents**: Stream of correlated victim reports with privacy-safe pseudonymous keys.
8. **Evidence**: Lineage explorer distinguishing original media artifacts from derived OCR/ASR extractions.
9. **Variants**: Historical split, merge, and mutation lineage tracking evolving campaign behaviors.
10. **Review History**: Complete audit log of all human analyst adjudication decisions.
