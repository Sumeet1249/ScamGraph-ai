# SCAMGRAPH AI — ANTI-MANIPULATION INTERVENTIONS (PHASE 4B)
## Deconstructing Social Engineering Tactics into Protective Actions

### 1. Tactic-to-Intervention Mapping

| Observed Tactic | Psychological Vulnerability | Protective Intervention | Key Consumer Message |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Authority + Fear** | Fear of arrest, criminal record, or court summons. | `TALK_TO_TRUSTED_PERSON` + `BREAK_CONTACT_AND_VERIFY` | *"Do not make a payment solely because someone is threatening legal action or arrest. Real police never interrogate via WhatsApp or Skype."* |
| **Secrecy + Isolation** | Cut off from family and institutional advice. | `TALK_TO_TRUSTED_PERSON` | *"If someone told you not to discuss this with family, bank, or police — pause right now and call someone you trust."* |
| **Urgency Pressure** | Artificially induced panic to prevent rational thinking. | `DELAY_PAYMENT` (120-min Cooling-Off) | *"Pressure to act immediately is a common manipulation signal. Take time before sending money."* |
| **Fake Reward / Greed** | Temptation of easy task profit or lottery win. | `DELAY_PAYMENT` | *"Small payouts were used to build trust. Never deposit funds to release previously earned bonuses."* |
| **Credential Pressure** | Phishing for netbanking credentials, PIN, or OTP. | `DO_NOT_SHARE_OTP` / `DO_NOT_SHARE_CREDENTIALS` | *"Never share OTPs, UPI PINs, or netbanking passwords. Legitimate banks never ask for them."* |
| **Remote Access Request** | Exploiting low tech literacy to take device control. | `DO_NOT_INSTALL_REMOTE_ACCESS` | *"Never install AnyDesk, TeamViewer, or unknown APK files for customer support or KYC updates."* |

---

### 2. Multi-Action Intervention Bundles

Complex social engineering situations require bundles consisting of:
- **Primary Action**: The immediate critical barrier (e.g., `DELAY_PAYMENT`).
- **Secondary Actions**: 1–2 supporting behavioral safeguards (e.g., `TALK_TO_TRUSTED_PERSON`, `VERIFY_INDEPENDENTLY`).

Example for Digital Arrest:
- Primary: `TALK_TO_TRUSTED_PERSON`
- Secondaries: `BREAK_CONTACT_AND_VERIFY`, `VERIFY_INDEPENDENTLY`, `DELAY_PAYMENT`
- Friction: `LEVEL_4_STRONG` (120-minute friction cooldown).
