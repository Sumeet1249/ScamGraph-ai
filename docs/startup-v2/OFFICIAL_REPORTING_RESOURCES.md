# Official Reporting Resources Registry (India-First) (Phase 5B)

## Verified Contact Registry
Official reporting information is maintained in an auditable, verified configuration registry (`OfficialReportingResourceRegistry`) rather than hardcoded in UI strings.

| Entity | Channel | Official Phone / Portal | Role / Instructions |
|---|---|---|---|
| **MHA / I4C** | National Cyber Financial Fraud Helpline | `1930` | Inter-bank hold alerts along the money trail |
| **NCRP** | National Cyber Crime Reporting Portal | `https://cybercrime.gov.in` | Formal complaint registration |
| **SBI** | 24x7 Cyber Fraud Emergency Desk | `1800111109` | Digital banking lock & stop-payment alert |
| **HDFC Bank** | 24x7 Fraud Helpline | `18002586161` | Debit dispute & card hotlisting |
| **ICICI Bank** | Fraud Reporting Helpline | `18001080` | iMobile/Netbanking security lock |
| **Axis Bank** | Fraud Reporting Desk | `18004190068` | Account and debit freeze |
| **PNB** | Cyber Fraud Emergency Desk | `18001802222` | Digital banking block |
| **NPCI** | UPI Dispute Redressal Portal | `npci.org.in/.../dispute` | Formal UPI UTR dispute registration |
| **UIDAI** | Aadhaar Biometric Lock Portal | `myaadhaar.uidai.gov.in` | Biometric locking against AePS fraud |
