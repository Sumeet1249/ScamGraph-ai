# Console Privacy & Cross-Victim Anonymity

## 1. Principles of Analyst Access & Privacy Boundaries
Even authorized fraud investigators must operate under strict privacy controls:
- **No Victim PII on Shared Surfaces**: The Outbreak Radar, Campaign Explorer, and Knowledge Graph display pseudonymous incident identifiers (`inc_xxxx`) and aggregate metrics, never victim names, personal phone numbers, or home addresses.
- **Controlled Media Previews**: Sensitive audio recordings and private screenshot media are never preloaded broadly; access requires explicit inspection actions with ephemeral signed URLs.
- **Search Boundary Enforcement**: Search bars query known scam indicators (suspect VPAs, fraudulent domains, campaign IDs) rather than arbitrary victim enumeration.
