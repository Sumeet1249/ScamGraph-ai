# Alert Center Specification

## 1. Operational Threat Triage
The `/alerts` console manages high-urgency notifications generated by the Campaign Discovery and Outbreak Radar engines:
- **Alert Types**: `OUTBREAK` (rapid acceleration over historical baseline), `RAPID_GROWTH` (rapid entity/volume expansion), `RESURGENCE` (dormant syndicate reactivation), `EMERGING_CAMPAIGN`, `NEW_VARIANT`, and `HIGH_RISK_PAYMENT_NETWORK`.
- **Workflow State Management**: Alerts transition through `NEW` $\to$ `ACKNOWLEDGED` $\to$ `INVESTIGATING` $\to$ `RESOLVED` / `DISMISSED`.
- **Actionable Context**: Every alert exposes explicit metrics (velocity spike percentage, incident count window, primary observed channels, and languages) alongside direct links to the relevant Campaign and Knowledge Graph views.
