## 2. Representative Cohorts

1. **Older Adults & Retirees**: Susceptible to digital arrest, fake KYC, and pension utility frauds; tested on large text, simplified copy, and clear dial-1930 prompts.
2. **Students & Young Job Seekers**: Susceptible to part-time Telegram task scams, fake remote jobs, and loan app harassment.
3. **UPI-Heavy Smartphone Users**: Daily Google Pay / PhonePe / Paytm users facing QR collect requests and reversal scams.
4. **Regional Language Users**: Monolingual Hindi and Bengali speakers evaluating transliterated and localized advisories.
5. **Bank Fraud & Threat Analysts**: Specialized users evaluating false positive rates, candidate correlation recall, and Outbreak Radar freshness.

---

## 3. Pilot Directives

- **Ethical Boundary**: Never deliberately expose participants to live scammers or real monetary loss. Use high-fidelity synthetic scenario environments.
- **Privacy Boundary**: Telemetry collected during the pilot strictly excludes raw message text, credentials, phone numbers, and payment details.
