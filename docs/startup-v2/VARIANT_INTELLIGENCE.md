# Variant Intelligence: Scam Mutation Archetypes & Cross-Variant Recognition

## 1. Mutation Tactics in Indian Cybercrime
ScamGraph AI tracks five primary mutation archetypes deployed by organized cyber syndicates:

1. **Identifier Cycling (Zero Behavioral Mutation)**:
   - Syndicates swap SIM cards, WhatsApp accounts, and UPI VPAs every 4–6 hours while preserving script, journey stages, and extortion tiers.
   - *ScamDNA Response*: 100% match on Journey, Tactic, and Payment DNA.

2. **Narrative Paraphrasing & Language Shifts**:
   - Switching from English YouTube job offers to Hinglish product review tasks (*"mall orders review karke daily payout lo"*).
   - *ScamDNA Response*: Semantic and Tactic dimensions maintain high alignment; Journey and Payment progression confirm variant status.

3. **Platform Hopping**:
   - Moving from Instagram/WhatsApp to Telegram, Discord, or APK downloads.
   - *ScamDNA Response*: Infrastructure DNA detects the platform migration pattern without requiring static domain overlap.

4. **Authority Swapping in Extortion**:
   - Shifting pretext from FedEx/Customs narcotics claims to TRAI illegal SIM disconnection, Telecom money-laundering, or CBI arrest warrants.
   - *ScamDNA Response*: Identity DNA identifies the `LAW_ENFORCEMENT` archetype; Tactic DNA confirms `AUTHORITY` + `ISOLATION` + `SECRECY`.

5. **Financial Threshold Tuning**:
   - Adjusting first deposit demands from ₹5,000 to ₹4,750 or altering initial task payouts.
   - *ScamDNA Response*: Payment DNA models relative multipliers and progression behavior rather than absolute rupee quantities.
